Felix's Sounds

Summary of index.html (Felix's app) and app.js. Line numbers match the files as of commit 9707863.

How the pieces talk

Felix taps Play
→
openLesson()
→
speak() model voice
→
startRecording() mic
→
analyseTake() + starsFor()
→
finishLesson()
→
saveSession() Supabase or device only

Every failure on the way goes through trap() into the bottom error panel, with a plain hint.

Variables you can tweak

NameWhereWhat it doesHow to tweak it
TRY_COUNTapp.js 11Tries per lesson.5 by default. Lower for shorter sessions (10 minutes a day fits 5).
VOICE_RMSapp.js 12Loudness that counts as "sound".Raise if room noise counts as a try; lower if quiet voices are missed. Tune in M3.
PITCHapp.js 15Pitch multipliers for Lower, Same, Higher.Change 0.8 and 1.25 to make the pitch gap bigger or smaller.
PROGRESS_KEY, SETTINGS_KEYapp.js 13–14localStorage keys.Change the version suffix to reset everyone's saved progress.
starsFor() thresholdsapp.js 439–447200 ms, 300–2000 ms, hiss 0.12 and 0.2, peak 0.05.Make these gentler (lower hiss numbers) or stricter after calibration.
hissRatio() cut-offapp.js 358–3684000 Hz.Move down for softer S sounds, up for sharper ones.
state.settings.speedapp.js 18–31Default playback speed.Set to 0.75 for a slower default for Felix.
